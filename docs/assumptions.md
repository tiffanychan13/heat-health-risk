# Assumptions and open questions

## Working assumptions
- Existing cooling-center capacity, when activated, is large enough to affect outcomes for the highest-risk group.
- Forecast information is available 3–7 days ahead, enough to make a staffing decision.
- County/tract vulnerability indicators (SVI) are a reasonable proxy for population risk over the analysis window.

## Scope limits
- Claims are predictive/associational. Causal effect of activation is a stretch goal.

## Open questions
- Reporting completeness and lag in county-level ED-visit data.
- Availability of cooling-center capacity data beyond Phoenix.
